# PRODUCT.md — Pulkit Chaudhary, personal site

**Register:** brand (a portfolio; the design IS the product).

**What it is:** A personal site for Pulkit Chaudhary — Rutgers student (Data Science, CS, Math & Statistics; 4.0), Data 101 lecturer, quant/ML builder. The goal is to read as authentically *his*, memorable to a recruiter or engineer, and unmistakably not an AI template.

**Audience:** Recruiters, engineers, and collaborators who'll skim in 30 seconds, plus people who want the full story.

**The hook — pickleball.** Pulkit plays pickleball; the whole site is themed as a tournament program / court side. This is identity, not decoration: real court geometry, real scoring vocabulary, real equipment metaphors. It must feel like someone who actually loves the sport made this, not like a theme bolted on.

**Voice (three physical words):** athletic, precise, easygoing-competitive.

**Hard "no" list (learned the hard way):** no neon glows, no glassmorphism, no gradient text, no big-number hero-metric grid, no `01 · Section` numbered eyebrows, no tiny tracked uppercase eyebrow above every heading, no made-up skill ratings, no Pong.

**Hosting:** fully static Next.js, deploy on Vercel. See DEPLOY.md.
